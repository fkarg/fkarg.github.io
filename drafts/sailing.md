# Sailing

Over the last week (May 23rd to 30th), I went on my first sailing trip with an awesome group of people. I knew about half of them before this, now I know most of them a lot better. The trip was organized by a good friend whom I'm having weekly progress check-ins with and a lot of people distributed across Europe, most of which will also be at the LWCW.



A couple things I want to talk about:

- it was a 'traditional' sailing ship

- german <-> english in commands, crew comms etc was interesting

- awesome base crew on board

- full trip: Frankfurt -> Hamburg -> Eckenförde -> Kiel -> Malmö -> Wismar -> Frankfurt

- we had a delay and an emergency stop on the train to eckenförde, buying sailing gloves in the morning
