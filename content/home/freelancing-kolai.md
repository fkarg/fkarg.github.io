## Context

Kolai is a self-hosted, privacy-focused AI search and knowledge platform for European businesses. Development began around mid-2024, initially part-time. I held the Co-Founder & CTO role from June 2025 to November 2026.

## My role

I owned the technical delivery across backend services, deployment infrastructure, and integrations with LLM providers and customer systems. I led a small team of freelance and part-time engineers, and worked with customers through deployment and technical discovery.

The platform integrated with file shares, SharePoint, and Odoo, and supported LLM providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. Credential storage was encrypted.

## Results

- Served three paying customers across two production platform deployments.
- Improved answer quality on the internal QA benchmark from 35% to 93%.
- Reached p95 retrieval latency of about 9.5 ms and p99 below 20 ms for Q&A retrieval over approximately 370,000 documents.

These figures describe retrieval over the indexed corpus, not the number of documents returned in an answer.
