---
title: SWB Superpower (Electricity at margin cost zero)
---

In germany, we now somewhat regularly have summery days where electricity costs
are zero, or even negative. This is of course due to substantial wind and solar
contributions to the power grid.

This trend seems to also increase. It makes sense, generally renewables +
battery are the cheapest (new) source of electrity, though not always with the
quickest timeline from start to finish.

Regardless, if this continues it seems to me that RethinkX's idea of [SWB
Superpower](https://www.rethinkx.com/energy/in-depth/super-power), i.e.
superabundant electricity at near-zero marginal cost on most days is only the
logical conclusion.
