date: 2026-11-19

If we are in a simulation, how would one go about detecting intelligent life? most of our day-to-day activities and processes are just _regular_ biology / physics, with no extra 'cost' at sufficiently detailed simulation level.

I've spent a couple hours thinking and discussing it at this point. Have a think first, then feel free to expand my most promising options:

> [!example]- Spoiler 1
> near-zero Kelvin temperature spots smaller than any quasar

> [!example]- Spoiler 2
> small spots with insanely high local pressure or (heat) energy, in orders of magnitude lower environment

> [!example]- Spoiler 3
> high entropy radio signals, i.e. clearly non-natural ones carrying modulated signals

Feel free to write me if you think you found more / better ones, I'm always open for a discussion.
