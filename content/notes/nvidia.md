NVIDIA currently has an operating margin of 86%. That is insane, and a massive
flag for other companies that there's a _lot_ of value to be extracted by
providing competitors. NVIDIA used to be limited by fab capacity, but that
seems to be resolving now, with also TSMC operating margin increasing QoQ,
currently >60%, and ASML also >40%, partially due to each of their increased
lead times - and these are the major players getting down the stack, all other
related ones are acting similarly though.

Regardless, the point is that most of compute is currently trading at a premium
relative to even current _cost_, simply due to a value extraction overhang /
squeeze. This will resolve itself naturally over time, though it might take a
couple years for it to fully propagate. An operating margin of 80% for a purely
design-focused company is just too juicy for other (tech) players to refrain
from, and it seems that the cost where it starts to be worth it to create your
own silicone is a couple hundred million dollars. Given that all major playors
are currently investing double-digit billions per quater in compute, this
should pay off fairly rapidly.
